Predictive Modeling:

    The Decoupling of Engagement and RevenueTo determine the primary drivers of business ROI, we deployed two separate Linear Regression models to predict product_sales.

    Model 1 utilized raw top-of-funnel metrics (engagement, reach, duratio), while Model 2 utilized engineered, normalized rates (engagement_rate, daily_engagement_velocity, reach_velocity).

    Both models returned an R² score of effectively 0.00, with visualizations showing the models defaulting to mean-value predictions. 
    
    
This represents a definitive analytical finding: in this audited environment, social media interaction—whether measured by sheer volume or normalized velocity—is entirely decoupled from revenue generation. Optimizing for higher engagement rates or faster reach virality does not mathematically translate to increased sales.
